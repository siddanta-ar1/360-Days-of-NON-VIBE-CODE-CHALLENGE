The Concept:
An Architectural Audit is a structured review of your entire ecosystem. We categorize the system into four pillars and interrogate them for weaknesses, inefficiencies, and security vulnerabilities.

The Edge (Next.js & Nginx): Are our bundle sizes optimized? Did we leave console.log statements in production? Are our Nginx timeouts properly configured?

The Compute (FastAPI & Docker): Are our Docker images bloated? Is Python throwing silent warnings? Are we handling memory leaks?

The State (Supabase & Redis): Are our relational queries missing indexes? Are our Row Level Security (RLS) policies watertight? Does Redis have an eviction policy?

The Pipeline (GitHub Actions & AWS): Are our secrets rotated? Are the S3 backups actually working?
